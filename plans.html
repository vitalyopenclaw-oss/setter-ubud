Common Questions
Good to Know

How quickly can we move in?

Immediately. The space is fully equipped and ready — your team can start working on Day One. No fit-out period, no procurement delays.

Are there any hidden costs?

No. The space comes fully furnished and equipped. The terms you agree include everything — there are no surprise renovation bills or additional procurement requirements.

Can we lease just one floor?

Yes. Each floor (~360 m²) is fully self-contained and available for individual lease. You can also lease both floors as an exclusive full-building arrangement.

What lease durations are available?

We offer 1-year and 3-year arrangements. For longer-term commitments, we're happy to discuss tailored terms that work for your business planning.

Is internet connectivity included?

Infrastructure is fully in place. You choose your preferred provider and connect from Day One — no installation needed on your side.
